# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus criticism on the work, protect private financial information, and assume good intent while remaining clear about technical risks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
